# How to flash the firmware
 - Connect the Wemos to a PC
 - Go to https://web.esphome.io/
 - Click on `Connect`
 - Select `USB Serial` Port and `Connect`
 - Click on `Install`
 - `Choose File` browser for [petalot.v1.4.4.bin](https://github.com/function3d/petalot/blob/3ae9fc5ea56afeb9ac75492312b5a92eeb11acca/Firmware/petalot/build/esp8266.esp8266.d1_mini_clone/petalot.1.4.4.bin) and `Install`
 - Connect your phone to the WIFI "PETALOT-XXXXXX" and browser to 192.168.4.1
 - (optional) Enter SSID, password, IP address and subnet for your local WIFI
